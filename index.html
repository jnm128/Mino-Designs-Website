Frequently asked questions

Anything else, ask on the intro call.

What do you need from me to start?

A link to what you have today, a sentence on who your customer is, and access to any analytics. No formal brief needed.

Do you design only, or build too?

Both. I deliver Figma files your developers can build from, or build the pages directly in Shopify, Webflow or Framer.

Who do you work with?

Consumer founders, pre-seed to Series A, in wellness, beauty, lifestyle and food. Not sure you fit? Ask on the call.

How does pricing work?

Fixed price for sprints and audits, monthly for retainers. You get the number in writing within 48 hours of the intro call.
